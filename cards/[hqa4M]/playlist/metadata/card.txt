Author:: The Kanneh-Masons
Description:: The Kanneh-Masons are a family of seven talented siblings who love playing music together. ‘River of Music’ celebrates the sources of their musical inspirations: home, love and family. It includes classical favourites, folk songs, and spirituals.

The perfect introduction to classical music.    

“This is a story about our family and the sources of our music. Music runs like a river through the generations, and our passion for listening and playing music comes from our grandparents, and from their stories, flowing from the places and dreams they passed on” – The Kanneh-Mason Family
