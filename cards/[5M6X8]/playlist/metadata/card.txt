Title:: Storybook Time
Author:: Eban Schletter
Description:: Composer/songwriter Eban Schletter (SpongeBob SquarePants, The Patrick Star Show, Little Big Awesome) brings his unique and quirky sensibility to a trilogy of original stories. First, there are strange goings-on when it appears that a local lizard has set the neighborhood critters into action for a secret project. Then, after two kings end a feud, one holds a grudge that eventually grows into a kaiju sized problem. Finally, two space gods compete in an annual comet toss, and their rivalry takes an unexpected turn.  Told with wry humor, skillful scoring and sound design, these imaginative stories will entertain while teaching the dangers of holding a grudge, the benefits of admitting one’s errors, and the effects of following one’s bliss.
