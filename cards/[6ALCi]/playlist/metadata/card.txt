Title:: If Animals Celebrated Christmas & Other Holiday Stories Collection
Author:: Ann Whitford Paul
Description:: Narrated by Bradley Whitford, brother of the author and best known for his roles in The West Wing, The Handmaid's Tale, and Get Out, Ann Whitford Paul’s If Animals series, illustrated by David Walker, brings animals to life in heartwarming stories that imagine them doing the things we do. 

In If Animals Celebrated Christmas, Tortoise would hiss a holiday wish, Crane would croon carols, and Mama and Papa Buffalo would kiss beneath the mistletoe, with every creature decking the halls in their own way. In If Animals Gave Thanks, Raccoon chirps her gratitude, Crow loops and swoops in the sky, and Bear hosts a grand feast, capturing the spirit of Thanksgiving. In If Animals Celebrated Easter, the animals would join in to welcome spring, celebrating with joyous gatherings across the animal kingdom. For Halloween, If Animals Trick-or-Treated.

This adorable, bestselling series also includes If Animals Celebrated Christmas, If Animals Gave Thanks, and If Animals Trick-or-Treated, each story capturing the joy of family, friends, and togetherness.  

A Macmillan Audio production from Farrar, Straus and Giroux.
