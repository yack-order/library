Title:: Lullabies from Around Europe
Author:: Naomi Burrell 
Description:: This delightful second album of lullabies and night music from Naomi, Margareta and their Swedish friends will inspire your dreams and warm your soul. Relaxing you into a deep slumber through their rich collection of beautiful, European tunes, you will hear traditional lullabies in Swiss-German, Yiddish, German, French, Scots, and English, some translated poetry from Norway and even a spot of Shakespeare. The musicians improvised on their favourite melodies by Telemann, JC Bach, Bartok, Warlock, Schubert including a composition by Naomi and recorded it in a cosy studio in Gothenburg. Gentle spoken introductions paint scenes of moonlit skies, rustling leaves, sleepy animals, and drifting stars. A tender journey into the heart of night and the magic of dreams.
