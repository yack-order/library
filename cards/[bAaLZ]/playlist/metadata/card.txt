Title:: Ladybird Language Stories: My First Words in Arabic
Author:: Ladybird
Description:: Brought to you by Ladybird.  

We love learning new language skills. It can help us on holiday, and it can sometimes make our friends feel loved and accepted. On this adventure, we’re catching up with a pen pal who speaks Arabic. We’re going to learn some new words and phrases to help ask questions about what our friend like to do in their spare time.  

This interactive adventure is written to help young children confidently learn new language skills, while having some fun along the way. During the course of this action-packed hour children, will learn simple greetings, and range of Arabic phrases.  

This audiobook is aimed at children aged 4+, for family listening and learning and development for young minds.
