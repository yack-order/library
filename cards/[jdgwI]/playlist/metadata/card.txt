Title:: InvestiGators Collection
Author:: John Patrick Green
Description:: Narrated by a full cast, including author John Patrick Green and Christopher Hastings, InvestiGators delivers a fully immersive audio experience packed with lush sound effects and music that plunge listeners straight into the Gatorverse. From the bustling Batter Down bakery to the high-tech S.U.I.T. headquarters, the audio brings every scene to life as super spy alligator duo Mango and Brash tackle high-stakes missions.  

With over three million copies in print, this laugh-out-loud graphic novel series follows Mango and Brash as they surf sewers, use Very Exciting Spy Technology, and crack cases with their unique toilet-based travel techniques. In their debut mission, they face not one but two mysteries, while in InvestiGators: Take the Plunge, S.U.I.T. headquarters comes under attack, sending the duo undercover as sewer workers to capture the villainous Crackerdile. But a clogged toilet trap nearly sinks their mission—can Mango and Brash save the day and restore their reputation?  

A Macmillan Audio production from Roaring Brook Press.
