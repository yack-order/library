Title:: Guilds & Goblins - A What If World Collection
Author:: What If World
Description:: Get ready for epic quests, magical mishaps, and absurd surprises in these stories from the popular kids’ podcast What if World. Whether you’re a longtime listener or new to What If World, this collection is packed with imagination, adventure, and the kind of chaos only dice rolls can bring!  Join Zach, Scully the Squid, and Lola Rabbit and their characters: Zachimedes the egg-splosive goblin egg-chanter, Skullen Bones the re-arranging skeleton, and Lowlandra the fierce and fluffy were-bunny. Together, they embark on their first Guilds & Goblins campaign, led by game master Zizi.  These 12 episodes are a great introduction to the creative and collaborative world of tabletop role-playing games. They are ad-free and edited especially for Yoto.  Written & Performed by Eric O’Keeffe Artwork by Ana Stretcu.
