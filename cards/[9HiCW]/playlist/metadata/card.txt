Title:: Bedtime Stories with Nana Hannah
Author:: Nana Hannah
Description:: Bedtime Stories with Nana Hannah
Written, produced and narrated by Hannah Hellyer. Soothing stories to help your little ones drift off to sleep. Hello, and welcome — I’m so glad you’re here! Nana Hannah was born out of a nightly ritual with my own little ones — a cozy, peaceful way to end the day and ease into sleep. What started as a personal bedtime tradition has grown into a gentle, nurturing podcast designed to help children wind down, relax, and drift off with ease. Each episode of Bedtime Stories with Nana Hannah offers a calm and immersive experience, starting with a short, child-friendly guided meditation to help settle busy minds and bodies. From there, a soothing original bedtime story unfolds, accompanied by soft nature sounds, gentle music, and eventually fading into calming white noise. Each story is designed to create a peaceful environment and support a healthy bedtime routine. These 45 minutes episodes are thoughtfully structured to support sleep by including:
15 minutes of storytelling
15 minutes of calming music and nature sounds
15 minutes of white noise, slowly fading out as your little one drifts off to sleep   
This format mirrors the natural time it often takes for children to fall asleep, helping to guide them gently from wakefulness to rest — and hopefully giving parents a quiet moment too! Bedtime Stories with Nana Hannah was a finalist for best children’s podcast at the 2024 Australian Podcast Awards, and holds a 5 star rating on both Spotify and Apple Podcasts.  Tracks on this card include: Goodnight Oli the Octopus, Goodnight Bella the Butterfly, Goodnight Sadie the Star, Goodnight Parker the Pirate, Goodnight Fiadh the Fairy. Thank you for tuning in, I hope your little ones love these stories as much as mine do!
