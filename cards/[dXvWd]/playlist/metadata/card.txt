Title:: Big Emotions: Kids Listen Mashups About Feelings
Author:: Tal Kelly
Description:: Big Emotions: Kids Listen Mashups About Feelings is an audio series where stories meet feelings, featuring over forty trusted kids’ podcasts.

Each episode explores a pair of emotions—like Fear & Bravery or Jealousy & Openness—through immersive storytelling, music, and guided reflection, helping kids understand and navigate their feelings with curiosity and compassion.

Created with educators in mind and made for families everywhere, these Mashups make social-emotional learning (SEL) feel natural and engaging while guiding children toward greater confidence, empathy, and resilience.

Co-created by producer Tal Kelly, executive writer Jenna Clarke, and award-winning author and psychologist Dr. Eileen Kennedy-Moore, this one-of-a-kind series sparks meaningful conversations, builds empathy, and strengthens emotional connection in the classroom or around the kitchen table.
