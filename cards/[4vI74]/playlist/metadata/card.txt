Title:: Cornbread & Poppy
Author:: Matthew Cordell
Description:: Cornbread loves planning. Poppy does not. Cornbread adores preparing. Poppy does not. Cornbread is ready for winter. Poppy…is not. But Cornbread and Poppy are the best of friends, so when Poppy is left without any food for the long winter, Cornbread volunteers to help her out. Their search leads them up, up, up Holler Mountain, where these mice might find a new friend… and an old one.  This delightful audiobook brings their adventure to life with lively narration, making every moment feel as cozy as a warm winter burrow. A charming story of friendship and individuality, perfect for little listeners who love humor, heart, and a fun-filled tale to hear again and again.
