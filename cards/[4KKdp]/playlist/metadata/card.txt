Title:: Ladybird Language Stories: My Next Words in Spanish
Author:: Ladybird
Description:: Brought to you by Ladybird.  

We are going back to Spain on holiday! What are we going to see and learn next? What a great opportunity to meet new people and to learn some new words and phrases. On this holiday we’re going to be going to beach, enjoying some tasty food, and learning more about Spanish culture.  

This interactive adventure is written to help young children confidently learn new language skills, while having some fun along the way. During the course of this action-packed hour children will learn further Spanish skills focusing on buying and ordering, as well as making new friends.  They will have help from our expert guide and they will be learning some more brilliant facts about Spain too!  

This audiobook is aimed at children aged 4+, for family listening and learning and development for young minds.
