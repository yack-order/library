Title:: Morris and the Magic Bell 
Author:: Yoto
Description:: Follow along with the festive fun in this audio advent calendar. A new chapter unlocks every day until Christmas Eve! 

This holiday season, join Morris the bear, his new pal Whitney the rabbit, and the cast of zany characters they meet along their magical expedition to find Santa Claus and save Christmas. Put this Yoto Card into your Player each day of December to hear the next chapter in this festive adventure — the journey starts December 1st!

No one loves Christmas more than Morris, and when the first day of December arrives, he simply cannot wait to start decorating! While sorting through his countless boxes of wreaths, lights, and ornaments, Morris discovers one item that doesn’t belong to him: a beautiful, perfectly round, shiny golden jingle bell. Morris knows each and every decoration in his collection by heart… so who could this bell possibly belong to? Morris deduces that it could be none other than Santa Claus himself, of course, and without this magical bell, Santa’s sleigh might not be able to fly… and Christmas could be ruined for everyone! Oh no!

Being a very resourceful bear, Morris decides that – armed with his trusty Seeker Speaker directions device – he will travel the long and winding road to the North Pole to return the jingle bell directly to the big man himself. What could possibly go wrong?

FAQ:

How does it work?
Ensure your player is connected to Wifi. 

Put the card into your Yoto Player or Yoto Mini each day between December 1st and December 24th to unlock a new chapter of the story. 

Press or twist the button on your Player to find the correct door to open that day. The chapter number will match the date on your calendar. (No peaking! You won’t be able to listen until that day is here.) 

What happens if I miss a day?
	Use the right hand button scroll to the dates you missed and you can catch up. 

What happens after Christmas Eve?
The story is yours to enjoy forever! 

Is Morris and the Magic Bell available in other languages?
Yes. Morris and the Magic Bell is available to listen to in US English, UK English, and French. Please note that not all languages are available in every region.
