Title:: Cornbread & Poppy at the Museum
Author:: Matthew Cordell
Description:: Cornbread loves spending time inside. Poppy does not. Cornbread adores in-depth research. Poppy does not. Cornbread is thrilled to visit The Moonville Museum. Poppy… is not. But Cornbread and Poppy are the best of friends, so when Cornbread is invited to attend the Founders Gala to see the unveiling of a surprise new exhibit, Poppy agrees to be his guest. Their evening is full of spectacular treasures, including hand-carved hairbrushes, diamond-encrusted perfume sprayers, and a solid gold ice cream scoop. What other wonders will these mice discover?  With engaging narration that brings every detail to life, this audiobook is a delightful journey through an unforgettable night at the museum. Perfect for curious minds and eager ears ready to be whisked away on an adventure.
